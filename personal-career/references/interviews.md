# Interview and offer support

Existing applications enter directly through `support`; discovery/selection is not required to prepare an already active process. Fetch the application, original JD, **actual submitted CV**, recruiter correspondence/confirmed events, employer research and relevant evidence. A CV-library relation alone does not identify which version was submitted. If unknown, label the gap and avoid rehearsing unverified claims from the newest draft.

Build an employer-specific preparation pack: round format and rubric, the candidate's strongest defensible examples, likely technical/business questions, knowledge gaps, concise introduction and questions for the interviewer. Reuse relevant upstream `modes/interview.md` and `modes/interview/{plan,practice,debrief}.md` guidance after reading it, without importing automatic status updates or unsourced metrics. For coding, practise timed implementation and test edge cases. For architecture, explain components, data flows, state, failure recovery and tradeoffs with concrete diagrams where helpful.

Use question-by-question mock practice when asked. Separate factual accuracy, structure, depth and delivery in feedback; give a revised answer grounded in confirmed evidence. Mark suggested phrasing as coaching, never a new fact. Save useful lessons privately or in an explicitly owned Notion section.

After a real interview, record actual questions, candidate answers and feedback. Use `event-check` for confirmed events, retaining unknown dates as null. An interview completed is not a round passed. Rejection and interviewer identity corrections marked deferred affect local context without writing the tracker.

For offer/negotiation support, compare annual base, variable pay, sponsorship commitment, expected responsibilities, work pattern and progression. Use delivery and ownership evidence for the market case. An expected promotion remains expected. Help draft the negotiation; sending and accepting require the user's specific instruction. No system can guarantee interview-to-offer conversion.
